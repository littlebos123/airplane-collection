About Me

My name is Heather, a lover of airplanes of all sorts! Both of my parents are pilots and are captains for Delta Airlines. Though I have grown up around aviation, I am pursuing a degree in Graphic Design at the University of Georgia. Consider this a tribute: flight is still a significant part of my life and I am ever grateful for the experiences I have had.

Fun facts:

• My dad used to fly Boeing 747s until Delta retired them in 2017. He currently flies 767s (haha).
